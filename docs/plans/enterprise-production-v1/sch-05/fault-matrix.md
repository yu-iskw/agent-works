## Known limits

- An old worker whose lease expired but was not yet swept or taken over can still commit: its fence is current and the row is still `running`. This matches SCH-02 and avoids failing healthy slow steps; use `renew` to extend.
- A provider write that a stale worker completes after the sweeper voided its pending row stays `uncertain` (the late success report is dropped). Conservative by design; read-back or manual resolution settles it.
- `schedule_runs.status` is not mirrored from the worker outcome. `worker_executions` is the execution authority; the scheduler-writer guard (0026) makes a mirror a wiring decision.
