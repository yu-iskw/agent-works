# AW-M1 Beta: SMS reminder channel evaluation

Last reviewed: 2026-09-25. Owner: Care operations (synthetic test data).

- Cost: USD 0.012 per message; about USD 3,600 per month at 300,000 reminders.
- Measured reach in the August pilot: 94% of reminders delivered.
- Two-way replies supported, so patients can confirm or cancel.
- Regulatory note: messages must not include diagnosis details.
