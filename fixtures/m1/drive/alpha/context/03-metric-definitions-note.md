# AW-M1 Alpha: KPI definitions note

Last reviewed: 2026-09-30. Owner: Analytics (synthetic test data).

- Signups: count of accounts that completed email verification on the day, UTC.
- Active users: distinct accounts with at least one session on the day, UTC.
- Revenue: recognised subscription revenue in USD on the day, excluding tax.
  The authoritative definitions live in the BigQuery table metric_definitions.
